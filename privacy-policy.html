General information

This privacy policy explains which personal data is processed when you visit this website and when you use the waitlist form.

Personal data is any information that can be used to identify you personally, for example your email address or technical access data.

Hosting

This website is hosted by Vercel. When you open the website, the hosting provider processes technical data required to deliver the website securely and reliably.

This may include in particular your IP address, date and time of access, requested file, referrer URL, browser type, operating system and internet provider.

The processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is the secure, fast and reliable delivery of this website.

Server log files

When you visit this website, technical access data is automatically processed in server log files. This data serves technical security, error analysis and the stability of the website.

This data is not combined with other data sources.

Waitlist form

If you sign up for the waitlist via the form, your email address is processed.

The processing takes place solely to inform you about available capacity for personal 1:1 collaboration and, where applicable, to send you a link to a personal conversation.

The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, for example by email to info@lukasheck.com.

The form will be connected via an external form or email service. Once this service is finally set up, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

Google Fonts

This website uses the typeface Raleway via Google Fonts. When the fonts are loaded, your browser may connect to Google servers. Technical data such as your IP address may be transmitted to Google in the process.

The use is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is a consistent and high-quality presentation of the website.

For a more privacy-friendly setup, the fonts may be hosted locally in the future.

Cookies and analytics tools

This website currently uses no analytics tools, no marketing pixels and no non-essential cookies.

A cookie banner is therefore not currently planned.

SSL / TLS encryption

For security reasons, this website uses SSL/TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by "https://" in your browser's address bar.

Storage period

Personal data is stored only for as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as long as statutory retention obligations apply.

Waitlist data is deleted when you withdraw your consent or the purpose of the processing ceases to apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have in particular the following rights:

  • Access to your stored personal data
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure of your data
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Objection to certain processing
  • Withdrawal of consent given, with effect for the future

Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Among others, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg is competent.
