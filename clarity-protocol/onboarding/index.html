The small supplement reset

Your fresh start in week 1.

To begin, you take a one-week break from your supplements. This gives you:

  • A clear starting point for everything that follows
  • A breather for your body. It may help to relieve your liver and gut.
  • More reliable blood values, for example without distortion from biotin
  • A better overview of what you really need. This can save effort and money.
  • A targeted fresh start, after which you bring products back step by step

It's that simple

  1. One week off all supplements, except omega-3. It starts on day 2.
  2. Afterwards, if you like, you get a fresh blood test.
  3. Then you bring your products back step by step.
If you are already a client and current blood values are available: 3 days without supplements before the blood draw are enough, omega-3 excepted.

Of course, you keep taking medication and anything prescribed by a doctor.

Frequently asked questions
Do I have to get a blood test?

No. It is recommended but optional. You can start with the mandatory part.

What should I keep in mind if I get a fresh blood test with your biomarker list?

In Germany, the fastest route is usually as a self-pay patient via a local medical service or lab, or through your doctor. To make sure your values are meaningful, please note:

  • Time: ideally between 6 and 10 am, by 12 noon at the latest
  • Medication: keep taking as prescribed
  • Infection: better to cancel the appointment
  • 72 hours before: no biotin, no flights
  • 48 hours before: no hard training
  • 24 hours before: no supplements (omega-3 excepted), no alcohol, no training
  • 12 hours before: stay fasted
  • In the morning: 300 to 400 ml of water, no coffee, no tea, no caffeine
