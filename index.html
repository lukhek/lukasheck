FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do I have to do?

Three things: fill in the questionnaire, follow the plan, and give me feedback via WhatsApp. On request I order the products for you. I handle research, comparison and selection.

How much effort is it in everyday life?

The plan is built to fit your daily life: few products, fixed times, no daily check-ins with me. If individual lifestyle adjustments make sense, I keep them small.

Do I need a blood test?

Not necessarily. I include existing values. I only recommend a new blood draw if it supports a decision.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

If you’re not satisfied at the end, you write to me and get your money back.

Does this replace a doctor?

No. If you have symptoms or an illness, seeing a doctor is the first step.

I already take a lot. What happens to it?

I review every product. What makes sense stays, the rest goes.

What if I don’t need anything?

Then I’ll tell you.

How quickly will I notice something?

That’s individual; I can’t guarantee results. After 12 weeks we look together at what has changed.

How does the support work?

You write to me on WhatsApp whenever you like. I reply in batches, usually within 7 days.

What happens in the intro call?

15 minutes, free. We clarify your goals and whether the program is right for you.
